Profile

About me

I am currently a student in the Master of Arts in Public Policy with Certificate in Research Methods (MACRM) at the University of Chicago Harris School of Public Policy. I am interested in using data and causal evidence to expand educational opportunities and improve the design of social policies.

Before joining Harris, I led a research team as a Research and Project Coordinator at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile and Universidad de Chile. Our work included an experimental impact evaluation on rent subsidy utilization and public officials’ performance under the direction of Professors Pablo Celhay and Javiera Selman, funded by Chile’s Budget Office (DIPRES).

I have also collaborated with Professor Susana Claro on research about teachers’ growth mindset, educational monitoring during the COVID-19 pandemic, and more inclusive reporting of national learning outcomes.

Previously, I worked with Christopher Neilson and Francisco Gallego at J-PAL LAC and later at ConsiliumBots on large-scale education projects in Chile and Colombia, including Decidiendo un Futuro Mejor and a nationwide randomized evaluation of smart matching platforms.

I hold a Master’s Degree in Public Policy (2020–2021) and a B.S. in Civil Engineering of Industry (2014–2019), both from Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile.
